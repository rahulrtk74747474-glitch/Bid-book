## Provider promises

The provider agrees to:

- maintain truthful profile, business, service-area, experience and verification information;
- offer only services it is legally and practically qualified to perform;
- maintain licences, registrations, insurance and safety practices required for its work;
- ensure authorized staff/technicians follow the same obligations;
- clearly state pricing, taxes, materials, exclusions and warranty terms;
- perform accepted work professionally and safely;
- protect customer property, data and addresses;
- use booking/chat/payment flows honestly;
- not manipulate bid history, reviews, referrals, accounts, group votes or payments;
- cooperate with legitimate support, warranty, safety and dispute investigations.

## Staff and technician assignment

A company account may authorize managers, dispatchers, technicians and accountants. The provider remains responsible for access it grants. Only an authorized/assigned worker should attend and start a protected booking.

The assigned provider/technician must obtain the customer's short-lived Bid&Book start code at the job site before protected work begins. The code must not be requested in advance or reused.

## Bids

A submitted or revised bid is a commercial offer. Revised bids create new immutable history events; they do not erase prior offers. Providers must not coordinate with competitors to manipulate price or bidding outcomes.

## Payments and payouts

Provider earnings are derived from the server-side booking/payment record, less any disclosed marketplace fee, refund, tax/withholding or other approved adjustment. Payout may be delayed/held while a legitimate dispute, risk, compliance or refund review is open.

The provider must connect a payout destination through an approved settlement flow and must never provide banking passwords, OTPs or card PINs to Bid&Book support.

## Additional work

If conditions at the job materially change scope or cost, the provider should document the change and obtain explicit customer approval before charging or performing additional paid work.

## Warranty

If the provider advertises or agrees to a warranty, it must honor the stated scope/duration subject to lawful exclusions. Warranty outcomes should be recorded in Bid&Book where the feature is available.

## Customer information

Customer contact/location information obtained through Bid&Book may be used only to fulfill legitimate marketplace transactions, support, safety or legal obligations. It must not be sold, scraped or reused for unrelated marketing without a lawful basis/permission.

## Suspension/termination

Bid&Book may restrict or suspend provider access for safety risk, fraud, material complaints, payment abuse, identity/business misrepresentation, manipulation or repeated policy violations, subject to the final published process and applicable law.
