Bid&Book is a marketplace that helps customers, independent professionals, businesses and neighborhood groups discover, request, bid for, book and coordinate local services.

## 2. Information we collect

Depending on the feature used, Bid&Book may process:

- account information such as mobile number, display name and verification status;
- provider/business profile information such as service area, skills, languages, experience, business details and availability;
- approximate or precise foreground location when a user actively chooses nearby search or provider service-area setup;
- service requests, bids and immutable bid-history events;
- booking, start-code verification status, payment/refund/payout records and transaction references;
- chats, support cases, reports, dispute/warranty information and user-provided evidence;
- ratings and reviews attached to completed bookings;
- device/session identifiers, push token and security/fraud signals;
- identity-verification status and provider reference where verification is used;
- files/photos/documents a user deliberately uploads.

Bid&Book is designed not to store card PINs, banking passwords, Aadhaar OTPs, fingerprints, iris/PID blocks or unnecessary raw Aadhaar numbers.

## 3. Why we use information

We use information to:

- create and secure accounts;
- match customers with providers;
- operate bidding, booking and neighborhood group-buying workflows;
- process and reconcile payments, refunds and provider payouts through approved external providers;
- verify identity/business status where required;
- deliver chat and notifications;
- handle support, disputes, warranties and moderation;
- prevent abuse, investigate risk signals and protect marketplace participants;
- comply with legal, tax, accounting and regulatory obligations;
- maintain backups, audit records and service reliability.

## 4. Location

Bid&Book requests location only when the user actively invokes a location-dependent feature. The mobile app does not require continuous/background location for its core marketplace operation. Providers can configure a service radius; customers normally see service area/distance rather than a provider's raw coordinates.

## 5. Identity verification

Identity verification may be performed by an approved external verification provider. Bid&Book should retain only the minimum result/reference needed for trust, support and compliance. The production implementation must not collect Aadhaar OTPs or biometric PID data into Bid&Book's own database.

## 6. Payments

Payments are processed by external payment providers. Bid&Book stores marketplace transaction references, amount/status, refund and payout records needed to operate the booking. Card credentials are handled by the payment provider rather than Bid&Book application servers.

## 7. Sharing

Information may be shared only as needed with:

- the other participant(s) in a booking/request/group transaction;
- payment/refund/payout providers;
- SMS, push-notification and cloud infrastructure providers;
- identity-verification providers;
- professional advisers, auditors or authorities where legally required;
- service providers working under appropriate confidentiality/security terms.

Bid&Book should not sell personal data to advertisers.

## 8. Retention

Define and publish production retention periods for account, booking, financial, bid-history, dispute, chat, support, verification and audit records. Some transaction/audit records may need to be retained after account deletion where required for fraud prevention, accounting, dispute handling or law. Deleted accounts should no longer be usable and unnecessary personal fields should be removed/anonymized according to the approved retention schedule.

## 9. User controls

The app includes or is designed to include controls to:

- review account information;
- manage notification/location permissions at device level;
- request an account-data export;
- request account deletion;
- contact support/privacy/grievance channels;
- report marketplace safety concerns.

## 10. Security

Bid&Book uses measures including TLS, encrypted secret management, short-lived access tokens, rotating refresh sessions, server-side authorization, administrator MFA step-up, rate limiting, audit logging, object-storage access controls and tested backups. No system can guarantee absolute security; material incidents should be handled under the approved incident-response process.
