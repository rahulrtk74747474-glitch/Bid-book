# Bid&Book Refund & Cancellation Policy — Launch Template

> Template only. Final commercial terms, fees, timelines and statutory rights require legal/accounting review before publication.

## Principles

Bid&Book should display the agreed booking amount before payment. Refund and cancellation decisions should be based on the booking state, provider/customer conduct, work already performed, materials or non-recoverable costs that were clearly disclosed, dispute evidence and applicable law.

## Before provider acceptance / before confirmed booking

Where no provider has been selected and no payment captured, a customer should normally be able to cancel the request without a service cancellation charge.

## Confirmed but not started

For a confirmed booking that has not started, the final published policy should specify any cancellation windows and any provider-reserved-time charge. Any charge should be disclosed before booking/payment.

The six-digit job-start code helps determine whether the service was actually authorized to begin.

## Started / in progress

After the customer start code has been verified, refund decisions may account for work performed, approved materials, documented scope and the reason work stopped. Providers must not invent undisclosed charges after the booking.

## Completed

After completion, customers may use applicable warranty and dispute channels. Outcomes can include:

- no refund / payout release;
- re-service under an offered warranty;
- partial refund;
- full refund;
- cancellation of an unpaid provider payout.

## Provider cancellation / no-show

The final policy should define consequences for provider cancellation/no-show, including customer refund, marketplace ranking/trust consequences and repeated-abuse review.

## Customer cancellation / no-show

The final policy should define reasonable consequences for late customer cancellation/no-show, including any disclosed reserved-time fee and repeated-abuse review.

## Group bookings

Neighborhood-group participants should pay through approved individual marketplace/payment flows. A group administrator should not casually pool or retain member payments. Refunds should be traceable to the individual payment/booking record whenever possible.

## Refund processing

Approved refunds are initiated through the original payment provider where supported. Bid&Book records the refund amount and gateway reference. Bank/card/UPI settlement time after provider processing depends on the payment network/provider.

## Disputes

Opening a qualifying dispute may hold an unpaid provider payout. Users should provide accurate evidence such as booking messages, approved scope, before/after photos, invoices and timestamps. Fraudulent or abusive refund claims may be reviewed as marketplace risk signals.
