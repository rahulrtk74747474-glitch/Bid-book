## 2. Marketplace role

Bid&Book provides technology for customers, service professionals, companies and neighborhood groups to discover each other, communicate, post requirements, bid, book, pay and manage service records. Unless expressly stated otherwise in a specific service, the service provider—not Bid&Book—is responsible for performing the underlying local service.

Bid&Book may provide marketplace payments, verification signals, support, moderation and dispute processes, but verification badges are not guarantees of skill, honesty, safety or outcome.

## 3. Accounts

Users must provide accurate information, keep device/session access secure and promptly report suspected compromise. One account may act as customer and provider where eligible. Businesses are responsible for the people they authorize as managers, dispatchers, technicians or accountants.

Accounts may be restricted or suspended for fraud, abuse, unsafe conduct, manipulation, repeated policy violations, unlawful activity or material risk to marketplace participants.

## 4. Customer responsibilities

Customers must:

- describe service requirements honestly;
- provide lawful access to the job location;
- communicate hazards or material conditions relevant to the work;
- verify the identity/company details shown in the booking before allowing work to begin;
- share the six-digit job-start code only when the assigned provider/technician is present and ready to start;
- approve additional charges only through the approved in-app process;
- use dispute/reporting tools in good faith.

## 5. Provider responsibilities

Providers must:

- provide truthful profile, service, pricing, business and verification information;
- comply with licences, taxes, safety rules and professional obligations applicable to the services they offer;
- send only authorized personnel to a booking;
- not start a protected booking without the customer start code;
- disclose inclusions, exclusions, materials, taxes, warranty and additional-charge conditions clearly;
- protect customer property and privacy;
- not manipulate bids, ratings, reviews, referrals or accounts.

## 6. Bidding

Bid&Book uses a transparent append-only bid history for bidding-enabled requests. A provider's revised bid creates a new event and does not erase the older bid event. Only the provider's current/latest valid offer is awardable unless the product explicitly states otherwise.

The booking records the exact accepted bid-event ID and agreed amount. Later bid events cannot modify the booked price.

## 7. Neighborhood groups

Group administrators may organize proposals, member voting and aggregated requirements. Unless an approved regulated payment arrangement expressly provides otherwise, group administrators must not collect or hold pooled member funds on behalf of Bid&Book. Members should pay their own approved portions through the marketplace/payment provider.

Group administrators must use their role in good faith and must not manipulate member votes or provider bidding.

## 8. Pricing and additional charges

The booking amount displayed in Bid&Book is derived from the direct listing or accepted bid. Providers may not demand unapproved off-platform additions as a condition of performing already-booked work. Material scope changes should be documented and explicitly approved before extra work is performed.

## 9. Payments, refunds and payouts

Payments may be processed by an external regulated/approved payment provider. Bid&Book does not store card PINs or banking passwords. Provider payouts may be held where a dispute, refund, compliance concern or material risk review is open.

Refund eligibility is governed by the final Refund/Cancellation Policy, booking facts, provider terms/warranty and applicable law.

## 10. Reviews

Only eligible completed marketplace bookings may produce transaction-linked reviews. Reviews must reflect genuine experience and must not contain unlawful content, threats, harassment, fabricated claims or unrelated personal information. Bid&Book may moderate content while preserving legitimate critical feedback.

## 11. Safety and prohibited conduct

Users must not use Bid&Book for unlawful services, scams, harassment, violence, identity fraud, payment fraud, account selling, bid collusion, fake bookings/reviews, malware, exploitation or other harmful activity.

For services presenting meaningful physical/safety risk, users remain responsible for taking reasonable real-world precautions and complying with applicable licensing/safety requirements.

## 12. Communications and privacy

Users should keep service coordination within Bid&Book chat where practical so booking records can support safety and dispute handling. Personal information obtained through the marketplace may only be used for legitimate transaction purposes.

## 13. Warranty and disputes

Where a provider offers a warranty, the applicable warranty scope/duration should be stated in the booking or service offer. Users may submit warranty claims and disputes with supporting evidence. Bid&Book may hold unpaid provider payouts during review and may facilitate outcomes such as release, re-service, partial refund or full refund where supported by the applicable terms and law.
